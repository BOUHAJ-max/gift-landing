05 · READING THE NUMBERS

What a good keyword looks like

Use these as a starting point, then adjust the rules to your own niche and budget.

GO

Publish on it

Found in autocomplete, under about 1,000 results, several books under BSR 100,000, and some of them with fewer than 50 reviews.

WATCH

Use it carefully

Real demand but one weak point, often heavy competition. Use it as a subtitle or backend keyword, or look for a narrower version with the Miner.

SKIP

Move on

No proven demand, or a niche held by books with thousands of reviews. Your time is better spent on the next keyword.

07 · FAQ

Questions

Which Amazon marketplaces does it support?

Amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .es, .it and .ca, for both paperback and Kindle searches.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay $39 once. The optional Claude AI Analyst uses your own Anthropic API key, billed by Anthropic only if you choose to use it.

Why isn't it on the Chrome Web Store?

It is sold directly so you pay once with no store markup. Installing from the folder takes two minutes with the included guide, and works the same way.

Will Amazon block my account?

IRIDIUM only reads public search and product pages, the same ones you open yourself, with pauses between pages. It never touches your KDP account. If Amazon shows a check page, the queue pauses until you solve it.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser, on your computer. Nothing is sent to any server of ours. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What if I change computers?

One license works on 3 computers. You can deactivate one in Settings and activate another, and move your data with a JSON backup.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If IRIDIUM doesn't work on your computer, you get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Refunded keys stop working.
